## Delivery

### Delivery Coverage and Courier
- We deliver all over Pakistan through TCS and Leopards Courier.
- We do not currently deliver outside Pakistan.

### Delivery Time
- Major cities (Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar): 3 to 5 working days.
- Other cities and remote areas: 5 to 7 working days.
- Orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours after confirmation.
- A tracking number is shared on WhatsApp within 24 hours of dispatch.

### Delivery Charges
- Delivery charges: PKR 250 flat rate for all cities.
- Free delivery on orders of PKR 5,000 or above.

## Payment Methods

### Payment Options
- Cash on Delivery (COD): available everywhere in Pakistan with an extra COD fee of PKR 100.
- Bank transfer (Meezan Bank).
- JazzCash and Easypaisa.
- We do not accept credit or debit cards at this time.

### Advance Payment Discount
- Advance payment (bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa) gets a 5% discount on the product price.

### Order Confirmation
- The order is confirmed only after the payment screenshot is received (for advance payment) or after phone confirmation (for COD).

## Return and Exchange Policy

### Return Window and Conditions
- Returns and exchanges are accepted within 7 days of delivery.
- The item must be unused, unwashed and have original tags attached.
- To request a return, send the order number and clear photos of the item on WhatsApp.

### Items That Cannot Be Returned
- Sale items, stitched or customized items, dupattas and scarves, and unstitched fabric that has been cut are not returnable.

### Exchange and Return Courier Charges
- Size exchange is free for the first exchange. The customer pays courier charges for any further exchange.
- Return courier charges are paid by the customer, except when the item is defective or the wrong item was delivered. In those cases we pay all charges.

### Refunds
- Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days after we receive and check the returned item.
- Refunds are made through bank transfer or JazzCash/Easypaisa. COD delivery charges and the COD fee are not refunded.

## Order Changes and Cancellation

- Orders can be cancelled or changed only before they are dispatched.
- Once an order is dispatched, it cannot be cancelled. The customer can use the return policy after delivery.
- Advance payments for cancelled orders are refunded within 3 working days.

## Custom Stitching Service

### Stitching Availability and Policy
- Available only for Summer Bloom Lawn 3-Piece and Winter Khaddar 2-Piece.
- Stitched items cannot be returned or exchanged.

### Stitching Charges, Time and Measurements
- Stitching charges: PKR 1,000 per suit.
- Stitching takes an extra 7 to 10 working days before dispatch.
- Customers must send measurements (bust, waist, hip, shirt length, sleeve length).

## Discounts and Sales

- 5% discount for advance payment on all products.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are your products original?**
Yes. All designs are made by our team and fabrics are checked before dispatch. Product pictures are real and taken by us.

**Do you have a physical shop?**
No, we are online only. We do not have a showroom at this time.

**Can I place a bulk order for a wedding or event?**
Yes. For 5 or more pieces, message us on WhatsApp for special rates and delivery timelines.

**Is the color the same as in the pictures?**
Colors can look slightly different because of screen settings and lighting. We try to match them as closely as possible. Customers can ask for extra pictures or a short video on WhatsApp before ordering.

**Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
Yes, but only before dispatch. Please message us your order number and the new address.

**What if my parcel is delayed?**
Please share your order number on WhatsApp and we will contact the courier. Delays can happen during Eid, rain or strikes.

**What if the courier does not deliver to me?**
Our team will contact the courier and try to reschedule delivery. After 3 failed attempts the parcel is returned to us.

**Do you offer gift wrapping?**
Yes, gift wrapping is available for PKR 300.
